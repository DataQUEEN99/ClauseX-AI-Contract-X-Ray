# ClauseX — AI Contract X-Ray

> **See the clauses that deserve a second look.**

ClauseX is an AI-powered contract intelligence platform that transforms complex contracts into a visual **Contract X-Ray**.

Instead of reading an entire agreement and trying to identify important terms manually, ClauseX analyzes the document clause-by-clause, highlights potentially concerning terms, explains them in plain language, and provides practical questions users can consider before signing.

---

##  Overview

Contracts often contain important terms around:

- Early termination
- Penalties
- Payment obligations
- Automatic renewal
- Liability
- Deposits
- Notice periods
- Privacy and data usage
- Intellectual property
- Confidentiality
- Dispute resolution

These clauses can be difficult to identify and understand, especially when they are buried inside long documents.

**ClauseX turns the contract into an interactive visual risk map.**
